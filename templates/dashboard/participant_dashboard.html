The Phantasia Research Hub is organised into different domains, each exploring a specific aspect of cognition and how it relates to mental imagery.

You're free to complete measures in any order. However, we have a 'core' set of questionnaires that we recommend you start with. These are grouped at the top of the list, each with the red marker. Or you can just start with whichever domain interests you most, or work through the available questionnaires and tasks at your own pace.

Each measure takes between 1 and 10 minutes on average.
